About us

Welcome to Paw & Habitat Care, your trusted partner in comprehensive, compassionate pet sitting and habitat maintenance. We understand that your pets are family, whether they have fur, feathers, fins, or scales. Our all-inclusive daily care service ensures your companion receives undivided attention, nutritious meals, exercise, and affection right in the comfort of their home environment. By offering a straightforward daily rate, we take the stress out of your travel plans or busy work schedules, giving you complete peace of mind knowing your beloved pets are receiving attentive, high-quality care tailored to their unique routines.


Beyond everyday companionship, we specialize in complete pet and habitat deep-cleaning services to keep your home fresh and healthy. From detailing cat litter boxes, small animal enclosures, and bird cages to specialized aquarium and terrarium maintenance, our trained staff handles the messy work with professional care. We utilize pet-safe, non-toxic sanitizing products to eliminate odors, scrub surfaces, and balance aquatic environments. Whether you need a standard tank scrub and water change or a full cage refresh, we ensure your pet’s living space remains pristine, hygienic, and safe.


At Paw & Habitat Care, our mission is to deliver exceptional, reliable service that keeps your pets thriving and your home spotless. We pride ourselves on clear communication, custom care plans, and strict adherence to your specific instructions. Let us take care of the day-to-day feeding, playtime, and dirty work so you can enjoy standard, worry-free pet ownership. Contact us today to schedule your initial consultation and discover how easy, professional pet care can be!
